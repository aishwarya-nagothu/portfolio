07 — Research & R&D

Exploring technology beyond the classroom.

AI · Sustainability · Battery Recycling
AI-Powered Smart Recycling of Lithium-Ion Batteries

My research work explores an AI-driven approach to improving lithium-ion battery recycling, particularly for the growing challenge of electric-vehicle battery waste.

The proposed framework combines X-ray-based battery identification with Machine Learning to classify battery chemistry before downstream processing, helping guide batteries toward appropriate recycling pathways.

X-ray Sorting Machine Learning Lithium-Ion Batteries Battery Recycling Direct Cathode Recycling Sustainability
